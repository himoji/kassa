# Compliance mapping

This document maps the requirements the submission must address to how Kassa addresses them, the code and test evidence, and a status.

**Status values**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Implemented** | The control exists in code and has a test that asserts it |
| **Simulated** | The control works in the sandbox with simulated counterparties. The production equivalent (licensed partner, real network, real list) is not present |
| **Planned** | Designed or partly built; not complete for the submission |

**Scope statement.** Kassa is a sandbox prototype. It does not hold customer funds, does not operate a wallet, and does not move real money. Custody, the payment service licence (EU PSD2 authorisation, or an e-money licence), and KYC and AML operations are out of scope. In production, a licensed bank or e-money institution would hold funds and run KYC and AML, and Kassa would integrate with it. Nothing in this repository claims otherwise.

Related documents: [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) (GDPR detail), [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (controls and residual risks), [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), [TESTS.md](TESTS.md).

Kassa does not operate in mainland China. In region CN it only prepares payments and hands off to the user's own QR wallet (`HANDOFF_REGION`, decide step 6), so it does not move money there. The table maps what a China launch would have to meet. It is the team's reading, not legal advice, and has not been reviewed by PRC counsel.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 6. OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025)

| ID | Risk | How Kassa addresses it | Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LLM01 | Prompt injection | Documents, emails, notifications and price lists are data. The Reader has no tools, no memory and schema output, and each field must match its source text (I10).
