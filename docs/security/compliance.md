# Compliance mapping

Requirements, how Kassa meets them, evidence and status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Implemented** | In code, with a test that asserts it |
| **Simulated** | Works in the sandbox with simulated counterparties; the production equivalent (licensed partner, real network, real list) is absent |
| **Planned** | Designed or partly built; not complete for the submission |

**Scope.** Kassa is a sandbox prototype. It holds no customer funds, runs no wallet and moves no real money. Custody, the payment service licence (EU PSD2 authorisation or an e-money licence), KYC and AML operations are out of scope; in production a licensed bank or e-money institution would hold funds and run KYC and AML.

Related: [security/privacy.md](./privacy.md), [security/self-assessment.md](./self-assessment.md), [architecture/overview.md](../architecture/overview.md), [reference/tests.md](../reference/tests.md).

## 12. Controls mapped to regulations (EU, Kazakhstan, China)

Team's reading, not legal advice; not reviewed by counsel in any of the three jurisdictions. Kassa holds no licence anywhere. In region CN Kassa only prepares the payment and the user pays from their own wallet (handoff), so the China column is what a launch with a licensed PRC partner would need.
